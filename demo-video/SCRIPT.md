# Cram Club: 2-minute demo, voiceover script

The script for **`demo.mp4`, the 2-minute cut**. Every time below is a real time in `demo.mp4`,
measured from the built file.

- **`demo.mp4` is 1:50.2** (110.2 s), 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264, **silent**.
  - It's made of 27 short pieces of the shot clips, one or two per feature. The edit list is
    `tools/cut-2min.sh`.
  - It was recorded on the live site as a guest, with the camera off.
- **Your face-tracking insert (about 8 s) goes at 0:50.7**, between demo mode and the shush.
  With it, the video is about **1:58**. Every time after 0:50.7 moves later by the insert's length.
- **The voiceover is 236 words.** At a relaxed 150 words a minute that's about 1:35 of talk.
  - Each line shows _words ≈ seconds needed / seconds available_.
  - Start each line at its time. If you read slowly, start a beat early, not late.
- **Ms. Fenwick is "the librarian", never "AI".**
- **The full-length version** has every clip untrimmed, with a longer line for each beat. It's
  [SCRIPT-long.md](SCRIPT-long.md) with `demo-long.mp4`.

## Record your insert (about 8 s)

The recordings never used a real camera, so this piece is yours.

1. In Chrome, open **https://cramclub.study/demo** in a normal window (not full screen), about
   1600 px wide.
2. On **Before you go in**, click **Turn camera on** and allow it. Then click **Go in**. If a
   "You're the first here" card shows, click **Not now**.
3. Hold **Shift**, click **Start pomodoro**, then click **Controls** to fold the demo keys away.
   - The **Your avatar** face view is in the bottom-right corner. If it's missing: Esc → Camera
     and face → "Show my avatar's face".
   - The timer card shows **Seen at desk**.
4. Press **Cmd+Shift+5** and choose **Record Selected Portion**. Drag a **16:9** box (for example
   1600x900). Under **Options**, set Microphone to **None**, then click **Record**.
5. For about 10 seconds: smile, turn your head slowly, nod, raise your eyebrows. Then stop the
   recording in the menu bar.
6. Turn the camera off: click the **Camera on** chip. Then drop the clip in at **0:50.7**.

## Editing notes

- **Jump cuts.** Pieces of the same clip meet at 0:09.0, 0:16.0, 0:30.6, 0:32.6, 0:43.7, 0:59.7,
  1:03.2, 1:11.2, 1:21.5, 1:33.4, 1:39.4 and 1:42.2. A 6–8 frame cross-dissolve on each makes it
  flow. The cuts between features (where the clip changes) are fine as hard cuts.
- **Blur:** the mic picker reads "Fake Audio Input 1" (the recorder's fake mic) from 0:11.5 to
  0:18.5. It sits at about x 1218–1465, y 740–775.
- **Who "you" are.** You build the lavender space-bun character, and she's back in the outro.
  From 0:47.2 to 1:36.4 the recording is in `/demo`, which gives you a ready-made "Alex". The
  0:47.2 line covers it.
- **Music.** The video is silent: add a quiet lo-fi bed under the voice.
- **Captions** (optional, if you add text on screen): "If nobody answers in 10 s, the librarian
  does" (0:32.6), "Only 8 numbers leave your laptop" (insert), "Rest day: one missed day in any 7
  keeps your streak" (1:29.4).
- **GitHub size.** `demo.mp4` is about 175 MB. GitHub's inline video limit is 10 MB (free) or
  100 MB (paid), so either export your edit at about 5 Mbit/s for 1080p (about 70 MB) or 720p,
  or upload it to YouTube and link it.
