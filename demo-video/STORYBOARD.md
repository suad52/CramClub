# Cram Club demo video: storyboard (source of truth for recording)

Target: about 5 minutes, 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent (the user records the voiceover and edits).
Recorded against the LIVE site https://149-248-61-2.sslip.io (production build: no dev badge,
real Gemini / TinyFish / ElevenLabs / Vultr / Tiger). Every shot is its own clip
(`clips/NN-name.mp4`) so the user can trim and reorder; `demo.mp4` is the assembled cut.

Rules for every shot:

- A visible cursor (injected) with a click ripple, moving smoothly (no teleporting), so viewers
  can follow. Calm pacing: hold 1–2 s on anything the voiceover talks about.
- No errors, dev overlays, half-loaded screens, or "Saved example" fallbacks in the final cut
  unless a live service genuinely fails (then re-record).
- Never the user's real camera or mic: Chrome fake devices only. Camera stays OFF in recorded
  shots (the fake camera is a green test pattern); the user records their own face-tracking insert.
- Student-facing words never say "AI": it's Ms. Fenwick, the librarian.

Each shot: what happens on screen, the PURPOSE it shows, and a draft voiceover line (the final
script re-times these to the recorded clips).
