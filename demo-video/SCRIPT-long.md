# Cram Club demo video: voiceover script for the LONG cut (demo-long.mp4)

The 2-minute cut and its script are demo.mp4 and [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md). This one times every line to demo-long.mp4 (every clip, untrimmed); "demo.mp4" below means demo-long.mp4.

The script you read while you edit `demo.mp4`. Every timecode below is a real time in
`demo.mp4`, measured from the assembled file; the clip-local time is next to it so you can find
the same moment in `clips/`. The shot list and purposes come from [STORYBOARD.md](STORYBOARD.md).

## At a glance

- **demo.mp4: 7:42.3** (462.300 s, 13,869 frames), 1920x1080, constant 30 fps, H.264, **silent**.
  All 15 clips in storyboard order, hard cuts, nothing trimmed. Recorded on the live site
  https://149-248-61-2.sslip.io (production build, real services), as a guest, camera off. After review,
  06, 07, 09, 10, 11 and 14 were re-shot on the same server, which by then answered at
  https://cramclub.study (the sslip.io link redirects there).
- **Voiceover: 879 words, about 5.9 minutes** at a natural 150 words a minute, so about
  76% of the runtime is talk and the rest is room to breathe. Every line was counted against
  its window: each row shows _words ≈ seconds needed · the window it has_. Start each line
  at its **VO at** time; if you read slower, start a beat earlier, not later.
- **Your own insert** (camera on, face tracking) goes **between 06 and 07, at 2:20.4**.
  Everything after that point moves later by the insert's length.
- Ms. Fenwick never gets called "AI" in the lines: she's the librarian. Sponsor tech is named as
  tech (Tiger Data, Vultr, Auth0, Gemini, ElevenLabs, TinyFish).
- Too long? See [Trims for a shorter cut](#trims-for-a-shorter-cut): about 51 seconds come out with
  no loss of coverage. (The storyboard aimed for about 5 minutes; showing every feature made it longer.)

## Your face-tracking insert (record this yourself)

The recordings never used a real camera, so this one is yours: about 15 seconds of the avatar
copying your face, and the "Seen at desk" line. **Put it between 06 and 07 (at 2:20.4).** 06 ends
on the desk view of a running pomodoro at Table 1 and 07 picks up at Table 3 with a fresh one, so a
short dissolve in and out hides the jump.

Steps on your Mac:

1. Open Chrome and go to **https://cramclub.study/demo** (the old https://149-248-61-2.sslip.io link
   redirects there). Keep it a normal window, not full screen: a MacBook display is 16:10 (with a notch),
   so a full-screen recording stretches or letterboxes when it's fitted to 1920x1080. Make the window
   wide, about 1600 px or more, so the page lays out like the video.
2. On **Before you go in**, click **Turn camera on** and allow the camera when Chrome asks. Check
   your face shows in the mirrored preview. (Skip the mic test; you don't need it.)
3. Press **Go in** (or Enter). You'll be seated at a table across from Theo (demo). If a
   "You're the first here" card shows, click **Not now**.
4. Check you see the red-dot **Camera on** chip at the top and the **Your avatar** face view in the
   bottom-right corner (if it's missing: Esc → Camera and face → "Show my avatar's face").
5. Hold **Shift** (it frees the mouse) and click **Start pomodoro** in the demo controls, then (still
   holding Shift) click **Controls** on the ribbon to fold the keys away so they don't cover Theo. The
   timer card shows **Seen at desk** with a percentage.
6. Start the screen recording: press **Cmd+Shift+5** (or QuickTime Player → File → New Screen
   Recording). In **Options**, set Microphone to **None** (you'll voice over separately). Choose
   **Record Selected Portion** and drag a **16:9** box over the page (for example 1600x900; the size
   shows while you drag), then click **Record**. (Or record the window and crop it to 16:9 in your editor.)
   The first time, macOS may ask you to allow screen recording in System Settings.
7. For about 15 seconds, look at the screen and: smile, turn your head slowly left and right, nod,
   raise your eyebrows, blink. Optional bonus: look away from the screen for 8+ seconds and
   Ms. Fenwick shushes you for real.
8. Stop with the stop button in the menu bar (or Cmd+Ctrl+Esc). The .mov lands on your Desktop.
9. Turn the camera off afterwards: click the **Camera on** chip → Turn off camera (or Esc → Camera
   and microphone).
10. In your editor, scale it to 1920x1080 (it's already 16:9) and drop it in at **2:20.4**.

Nothing but eight face numbers leaves your laptop while you do this (no video), so it's safe to
record; just close anything private on screen first.
